Account Deletion & Data Rights (Apple Guideline 5.1.1)

In accordance with Apple App Store Review Guidelines and privacy laws (CCPA/GDPR), you have the absolute right to delete your account and all associated data at any time.

In-App One-Click Deletion:

  1. Open DMV Practice Test: Smart Prep on your iOS device.
  2. Tap the Settings tab in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Select Profile Details.
  4. Tap Delete Account and confirm your choice.

Your account credentials, email, study streak, quiz history, and progress records will be immediately and permanently purged from our active systems.

Alternatively, you may request complete account and data erasure by emailing emircanmertt@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".

Frequently Asked Questions

Does passing the app test guarantee passing the official exam?

No. While our questions are modeled closely after state driver handbooks, the app is strictly an educational aid. Passing tests in the app does not guarantee passing the official state exam.

Can I use the app across multiple iOS devices?

Yes. If you sign in with your Apple ID or email, your study progress, bookmarks, and premium subscription entitlements automatically synchronize.
