We are dedicated to safeguarding your privacy in full compliance with United States federal and state privacy statutes (including the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA - "CCPA"), the General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR"), and Apple App Store Privacy Requirements.

1. Strict Governmental Disclaimer

THE APPLICATION IS AN INDEPENDENT EDUCATIONAL STUDY TOOL AND IS NOT AFFILIATED WITH, ENDORSED BY, OR CONNECTED TO ANY STATE DEPARTMENT OF MOTOR VEHICLES (DMV), DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION (DOT), OR ANY GOVERNMENT BODY. 2. Categories of Information We Collect

We only collect data strictly necessary to provide and improve the educational features of the Application. The categories of data collected include:

2.1 Information You Provide Directly:

  • Account Credentials (Optional): When you choose to create a personal account rather than study as a guest, we collect your name and email address.
  • Authentication Identifiers: When you use "Sign in with Apple", Apple provides an anonymized or user-selected email address and a secure, unique user identifier.
  • User Profile & Study Selections: Your chosen state (e.g., California, New York, Texas, Florida), applicant profile (e.g., adult 18+ applicant or under-18 youth permit applicant), and language preference (English or Turkish).

2.2 Study Performance and Interaction Data:

  • Test Results and Progress: Practice questions answered, correct/incorrect selections, study streak days, accuracy percentage, time spent per practice attempt, and questions marked as bookmarks/favorites.
  • AI Coach Insights: Algorithmic weak-topic analysis generated from your practice history to personalize your study recommendations.

2.3 Device, Diagnostic, and Technical Data:

  • Device Identifiers: Operating system version, device model, hardware specifications, app version, and anonymous session IDs.
  • Crash & Diagnostic Logs: Anonymized technical logs generated in the event of an unexpected crash or performance issue to help debug software errors.

2.4 Financial and Purchase Data (Apple App Store):

  • Subscription Status: Status of active subscriptions (e.g., monthly premium, lifetime unlock) verified securely via RevenueCat.
  • NO PAYMENT CARD DATA STORED: All financial transactions, billing details, and payment cards are processed strictly and exclusively by Apple Inc. via the App Store. Developer never receives, sees, or stores your credit card or banking information.

2.5 Advertising Identifiers (Ad-Supported Users):

  • For non-paying users, Google AdMob may process device advertising identifiers (such as the Identifier for Advertisers - IDFA, or Google Advertising ID) and approximate location (country/region level) for ad delivery, frequency capping, and fraud prevention, in compliance with your iOS App Tracking Transparency (ATT) and Google UMP consent choices. We request non-personalized ads only and do not use your data to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

3. How We Use Your Information

We process the collected data strictly for lawful, legitimate educational and operational purposes:

  1. Core Service Delivery: To authenticate your login, save your state-specific question preferences, synchronize your quiz progress across devices, and maintain your practice history;
  2. AI Study Personalization: To calculate your readiness score and recommend topics needing review;
  3. Purchases and Subscription Management: To unlock ad-free features and premium study tools upon valid App Store purchase;
  4. App Security and Fraud Prevention: To protect against abuse, bot activity, leaderboard manipulation, or exploitation;
  5. Customer Support: To investigate and respond to your technical support inquiries or bug reports;
  6. Advertising Delivery: To show non-personalized advertisements via Google AdMob (only where permitted by your consent choices) and award virtual study credits when you choose to watch rewarded ads.

4. Data Sharing and Third-Party Disclosures

WE DO NOT SELL, RENT, OR TRADE YOUR PERSONAL INFORMATION TO THIRD PARTIES OR DATA BROKERS.

To operate the Application securely and reliably, we share data solely with trusted third-party service providers who are contractually bound to strict confidentiality and security standards:

  • Supabase Inc. (Database & Auth): Cloud database storage, secure authentication, and encrypted progress synchronization.
  • RevenueCat Inc. (Subscription Infrastructure): Validates App Store purchase receipts and subscription entitlement status.
  • Google AdMob & User Messaging Platform (UMP): Delivers mobile advertisements to free users and handles regional privacy consent frameworks.

The Application is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under 13. 6. Data Security and Retention

6.1 Security Measures: We employ industry-standard administrative, technical, and physical safeguards—including SSL/TLS data encryption in transit and AES-256 encryption at rest—to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

6.2 Retention Period: We retain your account data and study history for as long as your account remains active. If you delete your account, your personal information is permanently purged from our primary databases within thirty (30) days, except where retention is strictly required by statutory or legal reporting obligations.

7. Your Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA / GDPR)

Depending on your state or country of residence (including California, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, and the European Economic Area / UK), you possess the following statutory privacy rights:

  • Right to Know and Access: You may request details on the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you.
  • Right to Delete ("Right to be Forgotten"): You have the permanent right to delete your personal data. You can perform this at any time directly within the Application by navigating to: Settings > Profile > Delete Account, or by emailing us.
  • Right to Correct: You may update or correct inaccurate profile information via Settings.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will never deny services, charge different prices, or provide a different quality of service because you exercised any privacy rights.
  • Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information: We do not sell personal data. You can opt out of cross-app tracking at any time through your iOS device settings (Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking).

8. How to Delete Your Account (Apple Guideline 5.1.1)

In-App One-Click Deletion:

In strict adherence to Apple App Store Review Guideline 5.1.1(v), deleting your account is simple, immediate, and fully accessible inside the Application:

  1. Open DMV Practice Test: Smart Prep;
  2. Tap the Settings tab in the bottom bar;
  3. Select Profile Details;
  4. Tap Delete Account and confirm your choice.

Upon confirmation, your authentication records, email address, profile, and all individual study histories are permanently removed from our active databases.

You may also request account erasure by emailing emircanmertt@gmail.com.
